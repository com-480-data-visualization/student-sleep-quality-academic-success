# Screencast script — COM-480 Milestone 3 (2-minute video)

**Hard cap:** 2:00 (penalised past 2:00). Target spoken time **~1:44** at ~150 wpm, leaving room for natural pauses between rows so the runtime lands at **~1:55**. Total script: **259 words**.

The narration walks the site top to bottom in viewing order, in one continuous take. No talking-head, no cuts — just the live page, the cursor, and the voice. All quoted numbers are verified against the CSV (n = 996) and match the process book.

---

## Part A — One-line concept

> Nine in ten students sleep enough — yet almost none feel rested or perform well — and the dashboard shows that **quality, stress and lifestyle**, not hours, are what move the needle.

## Part C — Recording checklist

- **Browser:** Chrome or Safari, 100% zoom, hide bookmarks bar and any extension chrome. Window: **1440 × 900** (matches the inventory done for this script).
- **Pre-set state before hitting record:**
  - Page reloaded so the hero is fully in view and the beeswarm has finished its initial layout.
  - Filter bar: every dropdown on **All**, no brush chips, **N = 996**.
  - Heatmap toggle: **Counts** (the default).
  - Stacked bar toggle: **Proportion** (the default).
  - Radar pickers: **Group A = Sleep quality / Very poor**, **Group B = Sleep quality / Very good** (the defaults).
- **Cursor:** move slowly and deliberately at all times. No flicks, no idle wiggles. When the script says "trace the cursor", that's a 1–2 second hand movement, not a flash.
- **Scroll speed:** for the scrollytelling section (0:11 → 0:53), use continuous two-finger / trackpad scroll — never a page-jump. Each step needs ~700 ms of dwell so the force-simulation can land before you scroll past.
- **Dry runs:** do **one silent dry run** with a stopwatch (or `/usr/bin/say -v ?`) before recording. If you cross 2:00, cut filler from the last two rows first ("with your selection", "lifestyle, not bedtime"). If you finish under 1:45, slow the scrollytelling section, don't add words.
- **Audio:** one continuous take. Headset mic. Plosive shield helps on "paradox" and "ninety-three percent."
- **After recording:** export as `.mp4` ≤ 1080p, upload unlisted to YouTube, paste the link into the README "Screencast" line on `main`.

## Part D — Emphasis cues

1. **0:25 — "Forty-six percent"** (Step 3 of the scrollytelling). The pink **46%** chip is right there in the prose card. Pause the cursor on it for half a second as you say the number — the audience needs a beat to register that the chip is the same statistic you're naming.
2. **1:20 — "routes through 'very poor' quality, then funnels straight into 'poor' performance"** (Sankey). Trace the cursor slowly along the thick ribbon while you say it. This sentence is the visual punchline of the whole video; if any beat needs to land on the eyes as well as the ears, it's this one.
3. **1:48 — "lifestyle, not bedtime"** (radar). Hold completely still — no scroll, no cursor — so the two overlaid polygons are the only thing moving in the viewer's head. Three words of silence on the visual is what makes the line feel earned.
